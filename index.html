Trophy Disc Printer

Put a logo on round sticker sheets and get a PDF that lines up. Print it at 100% and it lands on the stickers.

Edit measurements (mm)

Pitch is the distance from one circle's centre to the next. Margin is from the paper edge to the first circle's edge.

2 Logo

Add a logo and it goes on every empty sticker. Tap a sticker on the sheet to clear it, or to put the selected logo on it.

PNG or JPG. Blank edges are trimmed off automatically so the logo sits centred.

3 Print

First time on this printer? Calibrate once

This shifts the whole print on the paper to match your printer. It is not for positioning a logo; the arrows in step 2 do that.

1. and print it on plain paper at 100%.
2. Hold it over a sticker sheet up to a window. The crosses should sit in the middle of the circles and the line should measure 100 mm.
3. If every cross is off by the same amount, type that here. The setting is remembered.

Landed 2 mm too low? Enter -2 in Move down. Too far right? Use a negative Move right. If the line isn't 100 mm, the print was scaled: fix the print dialog, not these numbers.
