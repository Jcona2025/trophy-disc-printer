Trophy Disc Printer

Put a logo on round sticker sheets and get a PDF that lines up. Print it at 100% and it lands on the stickers.

1 Sticker sheet

Edit measurements (mm)

Pitch is the distance from one circle's centre to the next. Margin is from the paper edge to the first circle's edge.

2 Logos

Pick a logo here, then tap circles on the sheet to place it. Tap a circle again to empty it.

PNG or JPG. A logo on a white or clear background works best.

3 Printer nudge

Do this once per printer. Print the test sheet on plain paper, hold it over a sticker sheet up to a window, and see how far the crosshairs miss the circle centres.

Print landed 2mm too low? Enter -2 in Move down. Too far right? Use a negative Move right.

Tap a circle to place the selected logo. Leave used stickers empty.

In the print dialog choose Actual size or Scale 100%, paper A4. Never "Fit to page".
